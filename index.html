Times Black iShop strategy — how to maximise ₹2L annual threshold

Route regular spend through iShop vouchers

1Buy Amazon Pay / brand vouchers via Times Black iShop → get 12% reward on the voucher spend → then use the voucher for your regular Amazon purchases. Effective 12% on what would otherwise be 3–4%.
2Any shortfall to hit ₹2L annual? Push general shopping through iShop vouchers to reach the threshold and unlock ₹10,000 Klook voucher.
3Hotel via iShop (24%) vs HSBC direct (36%) — HSBC wins by 12%. Only use Times Black iShop for hotels if HSBC is unavailable or price difference justifies it.
4Flight via iShop (12%) vs HSBC direct (18%) — HSBC wins by 6%. Same logic — prefer HSBC unless price on iShop is meaningfully cheaper.
MRCC strategy — independent of other cards

Fixed monthly plan — ₹9,000 per card

1Buy ₹9,000 worth of vouchers per card per month → this counts as 6 transactions of ₹1,500 = triggers the 4× txn bonus (1,000 MR each card).
2If you can add another ₹11,000 on either MRCC card in that month (₹20K total), you unlock the second 1,000 MR bonus too.
3Keep each card at ₹1.5L+ annual spend → full fee waiver. At ₹9K/month × 12 = ₹1.08L — you need ~₹42K more/year on each card. Add some regular spend or larger voucher months.
4MR points never expire. Accumulate toward 18K Gold collection (₹9,000 Taj voucher) = ₹0.50/MR value.
Axis Atlas — Airbnb advantage

Why Atlas for Airbnb

AAirbnb qualifies as travel MCC on Atlas → earns 5 EDGE miles/₹100 → transfers at 1:2 = 10 air miles per ₹100 (10% return). HSBC gives only 3% on Airbnb (base rate). Atlas wins by 7%.
ADecision rule: if hotel price on booking.com/direct ≤ Airbnb price → use HSBC for hotel (36%). If Airbnb is cheaper → use Atlas (10%). The 26% rate gap means hotel needs to be significantly pricier to justify Airbnb.
APush Atlas to ₹7.5L annual for Gold tier → 5,000 milestone + 5,000 renewal = 10,000 EDGE miles = ₹10,000 bonus value.
